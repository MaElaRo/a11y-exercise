Ohm's law

Voltage, current and resistance are linked. If you know two of them, you can calculate the third.

The formula

U = R · IU equals R times I
U
Voltage in volts (V)
R
Resistance in ohms (Ω)
I
Current in amperes (A)

Rearranging it

To find the current, divide the voltage by the resistance. To find the resistance, divide the voltage by the current. A higher resistance at the same voltage means less current flows.

I=UR
R=UI

Check your understanding

A heating element has a resistance of 23 Ωohms and is connected to 230 V. What current flows?

0.1 A
10 A
253 A
5,290 A

How to solve it: I = U ÷ R = 230 V ÷ 23 Ωohms = 10 A.
